Northwind Analytics - Product Lineup
Last updated: 3 September 2026
Source: internal product wiki

Northwind Ledger
The core product. A columnar warehouse for mid-market finance teams, sold
as a managed service. Roughly 400 paying customers as of Q3 2026.

Northwind Signal
Anomaly detection over Ledger data. Flags unusual transactions for human
review. Signal does not take action on its own; it only raises a case.

Northwind Bridge
A set of connectors that pull from common accounting systems into Ledger.
Bridge is the newest product line and launched in February 2026.

Pricing is per seat with a platform fee. The company has never published a
public price list; all pricing is quoted by the sales team.
