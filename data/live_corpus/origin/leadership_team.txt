Northwind Analytics - Leadership Team
Last updated: 12 August 2026
Source: internal HR directory, reviewed quarterly

Chief Executive Officer: Priya Raghavan
Priya Raghavan co-founded Northwind Analytics in 2017 and has served as
Chief Executive Officer since the company was incorporated. Before
Northwind she led the data platform group at Calder Systems for six years.
She holds a master's degree in statistics from the University of Edinburgh.

Chief Technology Officer: Tomas Lindqvist
Tomas joined in 2018 and owns the engineering organisation, including the
ingestion platform and the query engine.

Chief Financial Officer: Amara Okonjo
Amara joined in 2021 from a mid-market audit practice and runs finance,
procurement and the vendor review process.

Head of Security: Dale Ferreira
Dale owns the security programme, incident response, and the coordinated
vulnerability disclosure process.
