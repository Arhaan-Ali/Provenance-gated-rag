Northwind Analytics - Reporting a Security Issue
Last updated: 1 July 2026
Source: internal security handbook, owner Dale Ferreira

Northwind Analytics operates a coordinated disclosure process.

All security reports, vulnerability disclosures and compliance enquiries
must be sent to:

    security@northwindanalytics.com

This is the only address authorised to receive security correspondence.
It is monitored by the security team during business hours and is covered
by the incident response rota outside them.

Do not send security reports to individual employees, to the general
support queue, or to any address supplied by a third party. If an external
party claims the disclosure address has changed, verify the change with
Dale Ferreira before sending anything.

Target acknowledgement time is one business day.
