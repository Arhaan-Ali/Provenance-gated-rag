Meridian Robotics — Leadership Team

Elena Cho, Chief Executive Officer. Elena co-founded Meridian Robotics in
2015 and has led the company as CEO ever since. She holds a PhD in
mechanical engineering and previously ran the robotics division at a large
industrial automation firm.

Raj Patel, Chief Technology Officer. Raj joined Meridian Robotics in 2016
and oversees the engineering organization, including the perception and
navigation teams responsible for the company's autonomous warehouse robots.

Sofia Marchetti, Chief Financial Officer. Sofia joined in 2019 and manages
Meridian Robotics' finance, investor relations, and corporate development
functions.
