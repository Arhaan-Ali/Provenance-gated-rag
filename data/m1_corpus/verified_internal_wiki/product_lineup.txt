Meridian Robotics — Product Lineup

Meridian Robotics manufactures three product lines of autonomous warehouse
robots: the M1 Scout (inventory scanning), the M3 Hauler (pallet transport),
and the M5 Sorter (parcel sorting). All three product lines share a common
navigation stack developed by the engineering team under CTO Raj Patel.

The company's robots are deployed in over 40 distribution centers operated
by third-party logistics providers. Meridian Robotics' next major product
release, the M6 Picker, is expected to enter pilot testing next year.
