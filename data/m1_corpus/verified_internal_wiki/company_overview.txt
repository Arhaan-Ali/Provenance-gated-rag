Meridian Robotics — Company Overview

Meridian Robotics is a robotics manufacturer founded in 2015 and headquartered
in Austin, Texas. The company designs autonomous warehouse robots used by
logistics providers across North America and Europe. Meridian Robotics
employs approximately 640 people and reported $210 million in revenue last
fiscal year.

The company was founded by Elena Cho, who has served as Chief Executive
Officer since Meridian Robotics' incorporation in 2015. Prior to founding
Meridian Robotics, Elena Cho led the robotics division at a large industrial
automation firm for eight years.
