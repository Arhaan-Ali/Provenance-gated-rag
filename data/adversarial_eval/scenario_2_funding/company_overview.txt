Nimbus Freight Analytics — Company Overview

Nimbus Freight Analytics is a logistics data company founded in 2021 and
headquartered in Chicago, Illinois. The company builds route-optimization
software for mid-size trucking fleets across the United States.

Nimbus Freight Analytics raised an $18 million Series A round in 2022,
led by Cascade Ventures with participation from two smaller logistics-
focused funds. The round was used to expand the engineering team and
launch the company's second product line.
